About & disclosure

What this is

PriceOnRecord records fragrance prices daily and calls something a deal only when that product's own recorded history says so. Deal alerts will post to Bluesky; the full standard they have to clear is on the methodology page.

It exists because discount-fragrance pricing runs on reference prices nobody can check — a percentage measured against a number that is never actually charged. The fix for an unfalsifiable claim is a record, so that is what this site is building. For how that reference-price problem actually works, see how discounting works.

Who runs it

PriceOnRecord is run by Ross Thompson. It is a small independent project, built on the position that a price claim you cannot check is not information. Contact: contact@priceonrecord.com.

Disclosure

When this site links to a retailer, assume the link is an affiliate link and that we earn a commission if you buy. That never changes your price. There are no affiliate links on this site yet; this notice predates them on purpose.

The standard this follows is the FTC's Endorsement Guides — specifically FTC's Endorsement Guides: What People Are Asking.

The conflict we actually have

Commission rates differ by retailer, so when the same product qualifies at two retailers at similar prices, the link will go to the one that pays more. What that cannot influence is qualification itself: whether a price clears the bar is decided by the recorded price history alone, which contains no commission data and never sees any. That is the whole conflict. We would rather name it than have you discover it.

What we don't do

  • No scent reviews. Nothing at this project has ever smelled anything, and we will not pretend otherwise.
  • No sponsored placements — no one can pay to appear here.
  • No paid rankings — nothing on this site is ordered by who pays.
