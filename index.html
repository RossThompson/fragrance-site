A fragrance deal is a claim. Claims need evidence.

Discount fragrance sites advertise 60–80% off nearly everything, nearly always. Those figures compare against a "retail price" that almost nobody charges — which makes them impossible to falsify, and useless. The only price signal worth trusting is a history somebody actually recorded. We are building one.

What will count as a deal here

A price will count as a deal when it sits meaningfully below that same product's own typical price over the last 90 days, at that same retailer — never measured against a list price, and never against a different retailer's price. It will also count when it is the lowest price we have ever recorded for that product at that retailer.

Either way, the product will need to be in stock, the price will be re-checked immediately before posting, and the same product won't be flagged again within three weeks. Deals will be posted to Bluesky, and each post will state exactly how much history backs it.

What we refuse to count

  • Retailer-supplied discount percentages — they measure distance from a price nobody pays.
  • "MSRP," list, or "compare at" figures — unfalsifiable by construction.
  • Any superlative our own recorded history can't back — "lowest ever" requires a record long enough to mean something.

The full standard is on the methodology page.

Collection has not started.

Prices will come only from official retailer product feeds. Feed access is pending publisher approval. Until at least three weeks of history exist for a product, nothing will be called a deal here.
